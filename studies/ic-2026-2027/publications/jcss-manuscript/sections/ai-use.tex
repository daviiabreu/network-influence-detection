\subsection{Use of generative AI}

AI agents were used throughout this project; the evidence table in the claim--evidence map links each role below to the records that document it. % M54
Claude models (Anthropic), recorded mainly as \code{claude-opus-5-5}, reviewed the candidate designs, exchanged amendment proposals and critiques with Codex before the v4 successor was adopted, challenged the focused literature review, ran the positioning search and audited the claim--evidence map. % M54
In separate sessions and from the specification documents only, Claude also wrote the two implementations used for docking (Section~\ref{sec:verification}). A Claude Code session coordinated the project, drafted amendment v4.4, wrote decision records and corrections, and integrated the drafts into this manuscript. % M55
GPT-6 models through Codex (OpenAI), recorded as \code{gpt-6-astra} and \code{gpt-6.1-sol} where identified, acquired, screened and synthesized the literature of the focused review, wrote internal adversarial reviews, and implemented the reporting, export, manuscript-assembly and packaging code. % M54
Claude and Codex sessions drafted and revised the text of every section, including the ODD appendix and the conditional Results, Discussion and Conclusion text, which was written before the confirmatory results were released; a deterministic program assembles the final text of those sections from it and the released values. % M54
Where a record does not identify the exact model, the evidence table says so. The internal AI reviews are model outputs, not peer review. % M54
The agents are not authors. The authors approved the study design and its amendments and take full responsibility for the content of this article. % M54
