X6b instead uses covered / completed successful cell pipelines. Name the success conditioning. |
| X6: `gaps_as_misses` denominator | Report this separately from primary coverage. Explicitly report repetitions without a curve, incomplete units and errors outside these denominators. Do not silently add those errors to a computed denominator or call this unconditional coverage over all planned repetitions. |

| X7: `far` | Write “gain relative to the frozen-feature LDA under the declared paired criterion”, with the actual boundary/ΔAUC predicate. Do not write “far from the information ceiling”. |
| X7: `close` | Write “local equivalence within the declared margin between the two LDAs near their common grid boundary”. This does not establish proximity to an information ceiling or Bayes optimality. |
| X7: `inconclusive`, `invalid`, `censored`, `unavailable` | Retain these states and their denominators. The population Bayes inequality does not turn a fitted empirical boundary into an optimal boundary. |
