## Coordinator decisions of 2026-09-29

Taken by the coordinating agent under the Owner's delegation, before any confirmatory result and any X3, X4, X6 or X7 result exists outside its seal. Each keeps the predicate the report code already implements, so no code changes. They are recorded as decision 82 of the Owner log.

- **Q8, per r.** The frozen basis asks for the complete and valid grids of the pertinent nulls, and for a comparison at one r the pertinent nulls are B and D at that r. The headline pairs B and D at every r where both units are complete valid and gap-free, and states how many r qualify and which. Requiring all 36 tier-2 units, or all nine D units, would let one failure elsewhere remove a comparison the text does not tie to it. With no eligible r there is no side-by-side sentence.
- **Q11, the whole gap-free grid.** "D tiver banda superior abaixo dele" names D's upper band without restricting strengths, so the band as a whole must lie below 0.70. Any reading of the form "at some strength" would be trivial at a = 0. The alternatives considered, D's band at the strengths where B crosses or at B's boundary, would label more units "compatible with non-Gaussian persistence"; the literal reading labels more of them inconclusive. Neither label claims partner drift, and the case where D's lower band also exceeds 0.70 stays under Q12.
- **Q16, no width threshold.** No interval width moves a unit into censored or indeterminate. The report README decision of 2026-09-28 stands, and the width against the one-step precision target is reported separately (B09).
- **Q22, no reversal wording.** The manuscript does not say that a sensitivity reverses the result. It reports, per r, whether the region of the sensitivity unit differs from that of main B, as the descriptive count the exporter emits; a region difference is not a significance test between conditions.
