## Unresolved evidence and decisions

1. **ODD reconciliation notes 1–12 are not in the repository.** The brief cites "nota 1" to "nota 12 do ODD", and `appendix-odd.tex` refers to "the authors' reconciliation notes". A repository search on 2026-09-28 found no such file. Every rule in this draft was instead confirmed directly in `src/ic_study/`. The notes should be committed, or the appendix pointer removed, before integration.
2. **Solver agreement count.** ID and the brief say 17/22 with five divergences. RECONSTRUCTION and the six cases listed in ID give 16/22. The draft uses 16/22. Research Owner: correct ID through a linked successor note, since reports are immutable, and correct the brief.
3. **Reporting separation of family-limit cells (M12).** No code separates screened family limits from solver or validation gaps in the released report; `fit_cell` only records the status and zero evaluations. Decide whether the separation belongs in the results exporter or only in the manuscript tables.
4. **"Sustained boundary covers reversals" (M24).** The brief says the sustained boundary "cobre a reversão" of the S09 hump. The draft reads this as: AUC reversals across strengths, whatever their source, are carried by a_s*. A hump-profile root is a valid accepted fit, not an AUC reversal as such. Owner review is needed.
5. **Main-curve rule versus AMD wording (M46).** AMD says a B failure "em qualquer r elegível" invalidates the main curve. `_close_inventory` has no eligibility exemption: any tier-1 unit that is not complete valid, including one invalid because its a=0 cell is infeasible, invalidates it. The draft follows the code. Confirm that this is intended.
6. **Supplement is stale on the stop rule.** `sections/supplement.tex` ("Gate accounting and execution") still says "a projected pass probability below 0.95 stops before G2". Code and AMD use 0.80, as do Methods and ODD35. The same paragraph block ("Protocol provenance") also says the amendment "remains a draft awaiting freeze and gates". Both need a separate edit; this drafter may not touch that file.
7. **The ODD appendix and Methods now overlap on band and seed detail.** ODD33 and ODD36 already give most of the band and seed rules. The integrating editor should decide whether Methods keeps the full band formula (M28–M29) or points to Submodel 13.
8. **AI disclosure scope (M55).** Adding the docking implementations to the AI-use statement changes the V22 disclosure. The authors must approve it, and `author-guidelines.md` ("Uso de LLMs e autoria") should be checked against it.
9. **Mac-side test run.** No record of a full study test run on the bound macOS host was opened. The claim that the three Linux failures pass on macOS is therefore not made.
