# Venue source notes for the manuscript pipeline

Checked 2026-09-27 against the journals' official pages. These are preparation notes, not a venue decision or a submission approval. Recheck the pages when the target and theme are chosen. The [JASSS submission page](https://www.jasss.org/admin/submit.html) loads its rules from an [official HTML fragment](https://www.jasss.org/v02/page_content_includes/_include_submit.html); the fragment was fetched directly because a non-JavaScript reader sees an empty container on the outer page. Short quotations below retain the source wording; the rest is paraphrased.

## JCSS — Journal of Computational Social Science

Source: [official submission guidelines](https://link.springer.com/journal/42001/submission-guidelines), checked 2026-09-27.

| Topic | Rule and location in official guidelines |
| --- | --- |
| Length | Full paper or short note; numeric word and page limits are not set. The journal cautions against text that is “excessively long” (§ General Information). A local page or body-word budget is therefore an editorial target, not a JCSS maximum. |
| Structure and files | Well-structured, interdisciplinary-readable article; no fixed IMRaD sequence stated. Title page includes author details; decimal headings have at most three levels. Springer Nature LaTeX with `[iicol]` is recommended and a compiled PDF plus editable sources are expected; Word is also accepted (§ General Information, Title Page, Text). JCSS uses single-blind review (§ Single-blind peer review). |
| Abstract and keywords | Abstract “150 to 250 words” and “4 to 6 keywords”; undefined abbreviations and unspecified references are excluded from the abstract (§ Title Page). |
| Data and code | Original research needs a “Data Availability Statement” explaining access to supporting data, including restrictions and conditions for sensitive data (§ Research Data Policy). Public repository deposit is strongly encouraged, not generally imposed. The declarations list material and/or code availability when relevant; no universal public code-deposit rule appears (§ Competing Interests, Summary of requirements). The methods and access instructions must allow replication (§ General Information). |
| LLM use | LLMs cannot be authors. Substantive use belongs in Methods, or another suitable part if Methods is absent; ordinary AI-assisted copy editing of human text is excepted, and authors remain accountable (§ Title Page, before Abstract). The operative phrase is “properly documented in the Methods section”. |
| Ethics and declarations | State applicable funding, competing interests, author contributions, ethics approval, consent, and data/material/code access before references; relevant omissions cause return as incomplete (§ Title Page, Competing Interests). Originality, no simultaneous submission, permissions for reused material, honest results, and relevant citations are required (§ Manuscript Submission, Ethical Responsibilities). |
| Figures and tables | Number and cite sequentially; put concise figure captions in the manuscript text; embed figures in the manuscript body unless upload size prevents it. Supply figures electronically and check legibility, resolution, accessibility, and third-party permissions. Tables need numbered captions (§ Tables, Artwork and Illustrations Guidelines). |
| References | Numeric citations in square brackets, consecutive numbered reference list, DOI as full URL when available. Only cited published or accepted works belong in the list (§ References). The examples' APA-like entries do not override the numeric citation instruction. |
| Supplementary material | Standard formats, explicit in-text citations, consecutive file names, and captions; supplementary files are published as supplied, without editorial reformatting. Data repositories are preferred where possible (§ Supplementary Information). |

The journal's stated assessment criteria are methodological and analytical soundness, a qualifying new empirical/theoretical/methodological contribution or broad-interest survey/data/software contribution, clear interdisciplinary writing, and enough data/method/analysis detail for replication (§ General Information, Criteria for Consideration). This supports the editor and methods review rubrics; it does not establish novelty for the current draft.

## JASSS — Journal of Artificial Societies and Social Simulation

Sources: [official submission page](https://www.jasss.org/admin/submit.html) and its [official submission-rules fragment](https://www.jasss.org/v02/page_content_includes/_include_submit.html), checked 2026-09-27.

| Topic | Rule and location in official submission rules |
| --- | --- |
| Length | Research papers and critical reviews in social simulation; normally “5,000 to 8,000 words”, plus hypertext attachments (§ opening). This is a typical range, not a hard maximum or minimum. No page limit is stated. |
| Structure and files | Five or six headed sections; authors should not number sections because JASSS numbers paragraphs in production (§ Headings and sections). Word or LaTeX is accepted, with figure files and bibliography; the journal recommends its LaTeX package (§ Submission format). The main file and preferably an anonymised PDF should be supplied; prose should avoid identity clues (§ The review process, Checklist for submissions). The prose first calls the blind PDF helpful but optional while the later checklist asks for it; preparing one satisfies both readings. |
| Abstract and keywords | The later dedicated section says “200 to 300 words” and “three and six keywords” (§ Abstract and keywords). It excludes literature citations from the abstract and asks for methods and main conclusions. **Internal conflict:** the opening instead says abstract at most 300 words and at most six keywords, omitting minima. Use the narrower dedicated-section bands for drafting, then recheck before submission. |
| Data and code | Authors are strongly encouraged to give enough information to replicate simulations; model code and documentation at CoMSES are recommended, and reviewer access may be restricted until publication (§ Replication and making your model public). ODD is recommended but explicitly “not a requirement”. The paper must be intelligible without code. No general data-availability statement or public data-deposit requirement is specified on this page. |
| LLM use | No LLM-use or AI-writing disclosure rule was located in the cited submission rules. This is **not** an exemption: disclose substantive use transparently and recheck current editorial policy before submission. |
| Ethics | Requires an originality/no-duplicate-submission declaration and points to COPE publication-practice guidance (§ Review, open access and publication practice). The submission page does not specify a universal human-subject ethics/consent statement, conflict-of-interest statement, or funding declaration. Determine applicability from the actual study and recheck at submission. |
| Figures and tables | Embed each figure in position and submit it separately, preferably PNG; caption text stays outside the image. Graphics should be at most about 800 pixels wide and remain readable in the reviewer PDF, whose conversion may truncate figures wider than about 600 pixels (§ Illustrations, The review process, Checklist for submissions). Tables normally remain text. |
| References | Modified APA author–date citations without the author/year comma; alphabetical reference list. DOI insertion is handled by the journal, so submitted references need not already include DOIs (§ Citations and References). |
| Supplementary material | Hypertext attachments are supported, but referees are sent only the paper PDF and no separate supplementary files. Put links to code or documentation inside the PDF if reviewers must use them. Videos and animation cannot carry essential meaning because only the first frame is visible in PDF (§ Replication and making your model public, The review process). |

The actual JASSS referee prompts ask for an overall assessment, a confidential reason for the editor, constructive author feedback, whether a simulation can be replicated from the provided detail, and whether the article uses electronic publication well (for example, code, data, colour figures, animation) (§ The review process). These are the strongest official anchors for the three-person adversarial kit. The page says at least three referees are normally approached; an internal three-person review remains preparation, not journal peer review.

## ODD second update: verified citation and scope

Grimm, V., Railsback, S. F., Vincenot, C. E., Berger, U., Gallagher, C., DeAngelis, D. L., Edmonds, B., Ge, J., Giske, J., Groeneveld, J., Johnston, A. S. A., Milles, A., Nabe-Nielsen, J., Polhill, J. G., Radchuk, V., Rohwäder, M.-S., Stillman, R. A., Thiele, J. C., & Ayllón, D. (2020). The ODD protocol for describing agent-based and other simulation models: A second update to improve clarity, replication, and structural realism. *Journal of Artificial Societies and Social Simulation, 23*(2), 7. [https://doi.org/10.18564/jasss.4259](https://doi.org/10.18564/jasss.4259). Metadata and the article were checked at the [publisher article](https://www.jasss.org/23/2/7.html) on 2026-09-27.

The 2020 article specifies seven numbered elements in three conceptual groups: Overview (1. Purpose and patterns; 2. Entities, state variables and scales; 3. Process overview and scheduling), Design concepts (4), and Details (5. Initialization; 6. Input data; 7. Submodels). Its figure and introduction say the numbered elements, rather than the three group names, define the protocol. It discusses eleven design concepts; inapplicable concepts may be omitted. A full ODD can live in supplementary material while the article uses a concise summary, but the complete version should exist first. Source: [publisher article, §§ 1 and 4](https://www.jasss.org/23/2/7.html) and its [official Summary ODD supplement](https://www.jasss.org/23/2/7/S2-ODD.pdf).

The JASSS submission page still cites the 2006 ODD and says the protocol is recommended rather than mandatory. Using the verified 2020 update for an ABM description is a manuscript-team choice, not a claim that JASSS formally requires this edition or ODD at all.
