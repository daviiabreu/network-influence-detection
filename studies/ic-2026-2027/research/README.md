method reviews and bounded exploratory records. The current paper is linked
from the [repository README](../../../README.md).

and [v4 amendment](questions/exploration/2026-09-24-parallel/detection-boundary-amendment-v4.md)

systematic map of the opinion-dynamics literature (protocols, search design,
run records and synthesis) is in [`literature/`](literature/README.md), and the
paper's positioning in [`literature/positioning-a-star-r/`](literature/positioning-a-star-r/).

Other retained discovery records describe their historical phase and limits;
extraction adds no scientific acceptance. Agent bindings, private governance,
execution logs and the venue search remain in private preservation.

monorepo, as explained in the
[provenance guide](../../../docs/reproducibility.md).
