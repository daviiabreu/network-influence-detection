1. Resolve the identifier policy and recover the absent title with recorded
   evidence, then compose the corpus from the completed, replayed acquisition
   and lossless preparation. Do not turn provider identities into an eligible
   study count or repeat the completed searches to change their outcome.
2. Prepare controlled publisher exports as a separately registered supplement, with

3. Build distinct tuning and verification cases from real works. Davi supplies

   exclusions. Known query-development seeds are not this verification set.
4. Execute the bounded role calibration, reproduce every receipt, and record
   Davi's actual 20-percent screening audit and review of every extraction case.
   Model agreement, schema validity and synthetic tests do not establish these
   scientific results.
5. Run the calibrated review in resumable batches, adjudicate conflicts and
   final exclusions, and obtain the owner's acceptance of the resulting synthesis.
